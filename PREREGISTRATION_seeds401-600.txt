PRE-SPECIFICATION FOR THE CONFIRMATORY BLOCK (seeds 401-600)
Policy I (absolute-scale RelayScore) vs. the other relay-selection policies

Save this file unchanged BEFORE running anything on seeds 401-600, and commit it
to git (or e-mail it to your supervisor) so that its date is recorded. Do not edit
it after the first run. In the paper, say it is a time-stamped plan, not a public
registration.

1. WHY THIS BLOCK EXISTS
   Policy I became the lead policy only after the fresh block (seeds 201-400) had
   been analysed, and on the development block (seeds 1-50) I trailed D by 2.8
   coverage points (q_BH = 0.058). Seeds 401-600 have not been used for any tuning,
   design decision or analysis. They give a test of I that does not depend on the
   earlier selection. [AUTHORS: confirm that seeds 401-600 were never used.]

2. SETTING (identical to the headline condition)
   Benapole Delta terrain, static wind, Np = 4, Nr = 4, 500 steps, theta = 0.5 for J.
   No parameter of any policy is changed or re-tuned on this block. Weights and
   prices are those already on disk: J (tuned weights of the paper),
   tuned_T_lite.json, tuned_V_lite.json, tuned_AUC_lite.json (lambda = 1600),
   tuned_I_lite.json, and the default RelayScore weights for C and I.

3. POLICIES
   Focal policy: I (default weights; no tuning).
   Comparators: C, D, D2, T, V (untuned, D's weights), AUC (lambda = 1600),
   B (nearest feasible), J.
   Reported but not used to confirm or reject a hypothesis: T_tuned, V_tuned, BR,
   I_lite.

4. METRICS AND STATISTICS
   Coverage (%), relay delay (steps), relay energy (%), seed-matched.
   Differences: paired Wilcoxon, BH correction over all difference tests of the
   hypotheses below. Equivalence: paired TOST, margin +/-2.0 coverage points
   (the margin used on the fresh block, which is not derived from a mission
   requirement), BH over the TOST tests. alpha = 0.05.

5. HYPOTHESES
   H1 (coverage equivalence). I's coverage is equivalent (TOST, +/-2.0) to that of
       C, D, D2, T, V, AUC, B and J.
   H2 (leanness vs. the untuned and auction comparators). I's relay delay and relay
       energy are significantly lower than those of C, D, D2, T, V and AUC.
   H3 (vs. J). I's relay delay and relay energy are not significantly higher than
       J's.
   Expected, not part of the confirmation (they were found on the fresh block and
   are reported as such): I's coverage is not equivalent to T_tuned and V_tuned
   (about 2.3-2.7 points lower); B has lower delay than I; BR has higher coverage
   than I.

6. DECISION RULES (fixed now)
   - If H1, H2 and H3 all hold: report that the fresh-block finding for I was
     confirmed on an independent block.
   - If any single comparison in H1-H3 fails: report that comparison as failed,
     name it in the abstract-level summary of the Results section, and weaken the
     corresponding claim in the paper. Do not drop the comparison, change the
     margin, change the policies, or re-select a block.
   - If H1 fails for the comparators D or D2 (as it nearly did on the development
     block): the paper must state that I's coverage equivalence to the FIS is not
     confirmed.
   - Results for I_lite, T_tuned, V_tuned, BR are exploratory and are labelled so.

7. WHAT WILL BE REPORTED
   The complete table produced by compare_focal_vs_all.py and the PASS/FAIL table
   produced by confirmatory_report.py, whatever the outcome.
