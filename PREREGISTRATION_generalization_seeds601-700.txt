1. WHY
   All headline and confirmatory results of the paper use one terrain (flat Benapole
   Delta), static wind and N = 8 UAVs. The earlier sweeps compared Policy J with D on
   the development block only. This sweep tests whether the LARS findings hold in
   other terrains, other wind modes and other fleet sizes. Seeds 601-700 have not been
   used for any tuning, design decision or analysis.
   [AUTHORS: confirm that seeds 601-700 were never used.]

2. DESIGN
   Part A (terrain x wind): 4 terrains (benapole_delta, sylhet_hills, coxs_bazar,
   chittagong_hill_tracts) x 3 wind modes (static, dynamic, stochastic) = 12 cells;
   100 seeds per cell (601-700); default fleet Np = 4, Nr = 4; 500 steps.
   Part B (fleet size): Benapole Delta, static wind; fleets (patrol x reserve) of
   2x2, 3x3, 4x4, 6x6, 8x8 (N = 4, 6, 8, 12, 16); 100 seeds per size (601-700);
   500 steps.
   Policies in every cell: LARS (RelayScore with absolute-scale coverage loss and
   default weights; Policy I in the code), C (RelayScore), D (true FIS), B (nearest
   feasible), T_tuned (TOPSIS with the weights in tuned_T_lite.json).
   No parameter of any policy is changed or re-tuned for any cell.

3. METRICS AND STATISTICS
   Coverage (%), relay delay (steps), relay energy (%), seed-matched within a cell.
   Differences: paired Wilcoxon with Benjamini-Hochberg correction. Equivalence:
   paired TOST, margin +/-2.0 coverage points (the margin used throughout the paper;
   note that it is relatively large in cells with low coverage), BH over the TOST
   tests. alpha = 0.05.

4. HYPOTHESES (primary comparators: C and D)
   G1 (coverage equivalence, terrain x wind). In every one of the 12 cells, LARS's
      coverage is equivalent (TOST, +/-2.0) to that of C and to that of D.
      Family: 24 TOST tests.
   G2 (leanness, terrain x wind). In every cell, LARS's relay delay and relay energy
      are significantly lower than those of C and of D. Family: 48 tests
      (12 cells x 2 comparators x 2 metrics).
   G3 (fleet size). At every fleet size N = 4, 6, 8, 12, 16, LARS's coverage is
      equivalent to that of C and D (family: 10 TOST tests), and its relay delay and
      energy are significantly lower than those of C and D (family: 20 tests).
   Reported but not used to confirm or reject (exploratory): comparisons with B and
   T_tuned; all other metrics.

5. DECISION RULES (fixed now)
   - A hypothesis counts as confirmed only if EVERY test in its family passes. For
     each hypothesis report the number of passing tests (for example "11 of 12 cells")
     and name every failing cell, comparator and metric, with its effect size.
   - A cell in which LARS is significantly WORSE than C or D on delay or energy is
     reported as a counter-example in the Results section, not in an appendix.
   - Do not drop cells, change the margin, change the policies or seeds, or re-run
     with other seeds after seeing results. If a run crashes, re-run the same cell
     with the same seeds.
   - If G1 fails in the hilly terrains, state that LARS's coverage equivalence does
     not extend to them; if G2 fails in a cell, state that LARS's delay/energy
     advantage does not hold there.

6. WHAT WILL BE REPORTED
   The complete output of analyze_generalization.py (every cell, every comparator,
   every metric), whatever the outcome.
